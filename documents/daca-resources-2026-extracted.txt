1. USCIS — Consideration of Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (I-821D)
The official USCIS page for the DACA program. Contains the current Form I-821D, filing instructions, fee information ($495 total for renewal), and eligibility requirements. The authoritative starting point for any renewal or inquiry.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. USCIS — Official Expedite Requests Guidance
Official USCIS page listing all qualifying criteria for an expedite request, including severe financial loss, emergency situations, humanitarian reasons, nonprofit organization requests, and U.S. government interest. Explains how to submit through the USCIS Contact Center or online account.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. USCIS — Congressional Inquiries Refresher Guide (PDF)
The USCIS internal guide prepared for congressional staff. Explains exactly how USCIS receives, processes, and prioritizes congressional inquiries — useful for understanding what your representative's office will do on your behalf and what to expect in terms of response timelines.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. NPR — Justice Department Makes It Easier to Deport Those with DACA Status (April 2026)
Reports on the April 2026 DOJ/Board of Immigration Appeals ruling establishing that DACA status alone is no longer sufficient protection from deportation. A precedent-setting decision affecting all current recipients.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. CNN Business — DACA Delays: Recipients Losing Protections as Wait Times Surge (May 2026)
Investigative piece on USCIS processing times jumping from ~15 days (FY2024) to 70+ days by early 2026 — a 400–1000% increase — causing recipients to lose jobs and work authorization while renewals remain pending.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. NPR — DACA Immigrants Approach Midlife, Stuck in 'Temporary' Limbo (May 2026)
In-depth feature on the human impact of ongoing DACA policy uncertainty, focusing on long-term recipients who have lived in the U.S. for 20+ years with no pathway to permanent status.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7. Presidents' Alliance — USCIS Policy Alert on Deferred Action & DACA Implications
Explainer on the May 8, 2026 USCIS policy memo emphasizing that deferred action should only be granted in "compelling individual cases." Breaks down what stricter scrutiny means for pending and future renewals.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8. National Immigration Forum — Policy Bulletin May 15, 2026
Weekly policy digest from the National Immigration Forum summarizing the most recent DACA and broader immigration policy developments. Useful for staying current on fast-moving legislative and regulatory changes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

9. DACA Renewal 2026: Fees, Process, and What Every Dreamer Should Know — Ilabacalaw
Practical attorney-written guide covering the full 2026 renewal package: required forms (I-821D, I-765, I-765WS), the $495 fee, stricter FBI background check requirements introduced in April 2026, and tips for accurately completing the criminal history and travel sections.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

10. DACA Renewal Timeline 2026: What Dreamers Need to Know — Novo Legal
Focused guide on timing strategy — recommends filing 120–150 days before expiration, explains how to track your case on the USCIS portal, and outlines what to do if your EAD expires while your renewal is pending.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

11. How to Expedite Your USCIS Case in 2026: Premium Processing, Congressional Inquiries, and More — Abogado Lozano
Comprehensive attorney guide covering all available expedite strategies: standard discretionary expedite request, premium processing, congressional inquiries, USCIS Ombudsman complaints, and Writ of Mandamus lawsuits. Explains when to use each approach and in what order.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

12. 2026 Guide for USCIS Expedite Request — Shoreline Immigration
Step-by-step guide on drafting and submitting an expedite request to USCIS: what evidence to include (medical records, employer letters, proof of financial harm), how to submit via the USCIS Contact Center or secure online messaging, and common mistakes that get requests denied.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

13. How to Contact Your U.S. Representative to Speed Up USCIS Processing — Boundless
Plain-language guide explaining how congressional casework inquiries work, how to locate your representative's casework office, what information to include in your request, and what to expect — USCIS typically responds to congressional offices within 30 days.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

14. DACA Resource Hub — Immigrant Legal Resource Center (ILRC)
The ILRC's central DACA hub, updated with a May 2026 practice alert on the new prosecutorial discretion policy. Includes free downloadable community guides, eligibility toolkits, and resources for both applicants and legal advocates.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

15. Why Some DACA Renewals Are Taking Longer — and What You Can Do — NILC
The National Immigration Law Center's practical guide for recipients experiencing delays. Covers how to document your situation, how to submit a congressional inquiry, and how to escalate if standard approaches fail. Includes a DACA renewal timeline calculator.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

16. Steps to Renew DACA — Immigrants Rising
Plain-language nonprofit checklist designed for students and young adults. Walks through each step of the renewal process in accessible language without legal jargon. Free resource with no attorney required.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

17. DACA Renewal Delays 2026 — TheDream.US (PDF)
(Downloadable PDF — March 2026) A focused report from TheDream.US scholarship organization documenting the 2026 surge in DACA renewal processing delays, their causes, and recommended action steps including congressional outreach and expedite requests. Specifically useful for students and scholarship recipients.

18. r/USCIS — Step-by-Step Guide: How I Filed a Writ of Mandamus
A detailed first-person Reddit post from someone who successfully filed a Writ of Mandamus to force USCIS to act on an unreasonably delayed case. Includes a step-by-step breakdown of the process, templates, costs, and responses from the community with follow-up Q&A.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

19. r/DACA — "Hey y'all, I did it — I filed a Writ of Mandamus"
A DACA-specific first-person account of filing a mandamus lawsuit after an extended renewal delay. The thread includes community questions and answers that provide additional practical detail on timelines, attorney costs, and outcomes. Especially useful for DACA holders weighing this option.

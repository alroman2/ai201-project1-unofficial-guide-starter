## 1. Official Government Resources

1. **[USCIS — Consideration of Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (I-821D)](https://www.uscis.gov/i-821d)**
   The official USCIS page for the DACA program. Contains the current Form I-821D, filing instructions, fee information ($495 total for renewal), and eligibility requirements. The authoritative starting point for any renewal or inquiry.

2. **[USCIS — Official Expedite Requests Guidance](https://www.uscis.gov/forms/filing-guidance/expedite-requests)**
   Official USCIS page listing all qualifying criteria for an expedite request, including severe financial loss, emergency situations, humanitarian reasons, nonprofit organization requests, and U.S. government interest. Explains how to submit through the USCIS Contact Center or online account.

3. **[USCIS — Congressional Inquiries Refresher Guide (PDF)](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/guides/Congressional-Inquiries-Refresher.pdf)**
   The USCIS internal guide prepared for congressional staff. Explains exactly how USCIS receives, processes, and prioritizes congressional inquiries — useful for understanding what your representative's office will do on your behalf and what to expect in terms of response timelines.

## Key 2026 Takeaways

- **Renewals are still open.** USCIS continues accepting renewal applications. New initial DACA applications remain blocked by litigation.
- **File early.** Submit 120–150 days before your current DACA expires given the surge in processing times.
- **Stricter vetting.** The May 8, 2026 USCIS memo introduced closer scrutiny on renewal applications — be thorough and accurate, especially on criminal history and travel history questions.
- **Deportation risk.** The April 2026 DOJ ruling means DACA status alone no longer guarantees deportation protection. Consult a licensed immigration attorney if you have any complications.
- **If your case is delayed:** Consider (in order) — (1) USCIS expedite request, (2) congressional inquiry, (3) USCIS Ombudsman complaint, (4) Writ of Mandamus lawsuit.

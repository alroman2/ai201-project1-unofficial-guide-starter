DACA Renewal Timeline 2026: What Dreamers Need to Know

Overview

This article from Novo Legal Group provides guidance for DACA (Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals) recipients navigating renewal in 2026. The piece emphasizes urgency due to ongoing legal challenges to the program.

Key Renewal Timeline

USCIS recommends filing between 150-180 days before expiration, with current processing times ranging from 3-7 months. Filing windows:
- 180-120 days: Ideal
- 120-60 days: Acceptable
- Under 60 days: Urgent
- Expired: Requires attorney consultation

Step-by-Step Renewal Process

The seven-step process includes:
1. Confirming eligibility
2. Completing Forms I-821D and I-765
3. Gathering supporting documents
4. Paying the $495 filing fee
5. Submitting to the correct USCIS lockbox
6. Retaining receipt notices

Critical Changes for 2026

USCIS is accepting renewal applications but is not approving initial (first-time) applications. Only existing DACA holders can renew. Additionally, advance parole for international travel faces stricter restrictions, and the program remains subject to federal court litigation.

Required Documentation

Identity documents, current/previous EAD cards, continuous residence evidence, passport photos, and fee payment are mandatory.

Important Advisories

The article stresses consulting immigration attorneys regarding criminal history, international travel, and potential alternative pathways to permanent status.

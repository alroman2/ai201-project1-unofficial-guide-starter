USCIS Expedite Request

Overview

The USCIS expedite request process will deliver a decision quicker than the standard processing time but will not guarantee that your application or petition will be approved.

Eligibility Criteria

Severe Financial Loss: Businesses experiencing significant financial hardship, including bankruptcy risk, layoffs, or lost contracts, may qualify. Inability to conduct U.S. business due to immigration delays can also support requests.

Emergencies and Humanitarian Reasons: These cases receive priority. Medical emergencies, disabilities, illnesses, and cases involving natural disasters or untreatable life-threatening diseases are considered.

Nonprofit Organizations: Tax-designated nonprofits can request expedited Form I-129 petitions when beneficiaries create positive social or cultural impact, such as doctors or scientists.

Federal Government Interests: Cases involving national security, public safety, or government agency referrals qualify when senior-level officials make requests and demonstrate critical need.

USCIS Errors: Clear processing mistakes by USCIS may warrant expedited resolution.

How to Request

There is no standardized form. Applicants should contact the USCIS Contact Center at (800)-375-5283, request an in-person appointment, or mail a detailed letter explaining their circumstances.

The guide emphasizes consulting an immigration attorney before pursuing expedited requests.

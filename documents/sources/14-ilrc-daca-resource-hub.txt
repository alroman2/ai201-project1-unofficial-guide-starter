DACA — Immigrant Legal Resource Center

Main Heading

DACA - Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals is a policy allowing eligible individuals to request deferred action grants, with options to renew and obtain work authorization.

Page Purpose

The ILRC's DACA hub serves attorneys, legal advocates, and community members with resources including program updates, policy changes affecting recipients, and immigration case assistance.

Featured Section

DACA Toolbox — Offers community-friendly information and free tools covering eligibility, requirements, application processes, and preparation for DACA submissions.

Recent Resources Listed

The page highlights multiple practice advisories and public comments on topics including:
- USCIS adjustment of status procedures
- Public benefits questions on address change forms
- Guidance for advising those leaving the United States
- FOIA rule changes and access restrictions
- Deferred action program trends and future outlook
- Information collection proposals affecting immigration applicants

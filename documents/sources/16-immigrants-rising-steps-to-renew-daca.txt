Steps to Renew DACA

Overview

USCIS continues accepting DACA renewal applications. Individuals with prior DACA approval may renew as long as they submit within one year of expiration.

Key Steps to Renew DACA:

1. Track Expiration Date — Find it on your I-797 form or work authorization card.

2. Know Your Renewal Window — Submit between 150-120 days before expiration to avoid losing protections.

3. Locate Previous Application — Use it as a reference for consistency.

4. Download Current Forms — Get Forms I-821D, I-765, I-765WS, and G-1145 from USCIS website.

5. Complete Application Accurately — Reference previous applications and USCIS instructions.

6. Seek Legal Advice — Consult immigration attorneys if you have criminal, immigration, or fraud concerns.

7. Create Cover Letter — For paper filings only; helps organize your application.

8. Prepare Fees — Online filing costs $555; paper filing costs $605 (as of April 2024).

9. Send Application — Use delivery tracking; follow correct document order and mailing address.

10. Track Status — Use USCIS online case status tool.

11. Attend Biometrics Appointment — Bring valid government-issued photo ID.

12. Respond to Requests for Evidence — Reply by deadline or face automatic denial.

13. Await Approval Notification — Processing takes several months; work authorization card valid two years.

Financial Assistance Resources

Organizations offering fee assistance and 0% interest loans include Mission Asset Fund, Self-Help Credit Union, and FindYourAlly.com.
